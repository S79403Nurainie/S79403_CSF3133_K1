About the Event

The Student Representative Council (MPP) of Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) proudly presents the Blue Economy Fun Run 2026! This isn't just any regular run—it's a massive movement blending fitness, sportsmanship, and marine conservation!

Experience 5 themed ocean checkpoints as you run:

CP1: Ocean Guardian (Plastic-Free Zone)
CP2: Deep Blue Neon & Bubble Station
CP3: Marine Life Zone
CP4: The Fisherman Community
CP5: Save Our Reefs Lane

Emcee: Azman and Sofea

Listen to an inspiring short briefing by our emcee.
