About Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially established on 1 February 2007, having evolved from a fisheries and marine science centre of Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM).

UMT focuses primarily on marine science, fisheries, maritime studies, and environmental conservation. Situated near the South China Sea, its strategic coastal location provides students and researchers with direct access to marine ecosystems and coastal resources for field research.

Over the years, the university has expanded its academic offerings to include science, technology, engineering, business, and social sciences, establishing itself as a leading institution in oceanography and sustainable resource management in the region.
